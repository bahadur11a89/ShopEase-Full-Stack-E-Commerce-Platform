Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days. Express delivery (ShopEase Plus members) takes 1-2 days. Same-day delivery is available in select cities.
What is the return policy? +
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on most products. Simply initiate a return from your account, and we'll arrange free pickup from your doorstep.
Are the products authentic? +
Yes! All products on ShopEase are 100% authentic. We have a strict seller verification process and quality inspection team to ensure product authenticity.
How do I track my order? +
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also track your order from the "My Orders" section in your account.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, EMI, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible orders.
How do I become a seller? +
To become a seller on ShopEase, email us at business@shopease.com with your business details. Our team will review your application within 2-3 business days.
