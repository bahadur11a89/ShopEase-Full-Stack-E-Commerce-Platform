Our Story

ShopEase was founded in 2020 with a simple vision — to make online shopping accessible, affordable, and enjoyable for every Indian household.

Starting from a small warehouse in Lucknow, we've grown into one of India's fastest-growing e-commerce platforms, serving over 2 million customers across 500+ cities.

  • Founded in 2020 in Lucknow, UP
  • 50,000+ products across 7 categories
  • 2 million+ happy customers
  • 500+ cities covered
  • 4.8/5 average customer rating

Our Mission & Values

We believe everyone deserves access to quality products at fair prices. Our mission is to bridge the gap between great products and great people.

We are committed to sustainability, customer satisfaction, and building long-term relationships with both our customers and sellers.

  • Customer-first approach always
  • Transparent pricing, no hidden charges
  • Eco-friendly packaging initiatives
  • Supporting local Indian sellers
  • Continuous innovation in shopping experience
